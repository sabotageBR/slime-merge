# Slime Merge: texto para o portal da CrazyGames (2026-10-19, versão 2.1.0)

## Short description

Drop squishy slimes into the jar and merge matching ones into bigger, sillier slimes. Can you grow the golden Super Slime?

## Description

Slime Merge is a cozy, squishy merge puzzle. Drop slimes into the jar, match two of the same size and watch them hug and merge into a bigger slime. Real jelly physics make every slime wobble, squash and take the shape of the jar, and each one has its own mood: happy, squished, sleepy, or grumpy when another slime lands on its head.

Chain merges for combos, merge three at once to skip a level, and climb 14 levels all the way to the golden Super Slime. Endless mode has no game over: every minute a new event shakes things up, and if the jar overflows, a quick rescue saves your run.

Features:
- Squishy jelly physics: slimes stretch, squash and slide down the glass
- 14 slime levels, from tiny drops to the golden Super Slime
- Boosts: Swap, Shake, Widen and a homing Missile, plus prizes like Heat, Freeze, Scissors and Paint
- Endless mode with a new event every minute: earthquakes, giant drops, heat waves, deep freezes, prism showers and slime orders
- Daily Challenge with a new twist every day
- Duel the CPU in a league, or play 2 players on the same device and send grey goo to your rival
- Shop: unlock jars, places, slime styles and skins, or draw your own skin in the pixel editor
- Daily missions and a 7-day streak

## Controls

- Mouse: move to aim, click to drop
- Touch: drag to aim, release to drop
- Keyboard: Left/Right arrows or A/D to move, Space (or Down/S) to drop
- Boosts: click or tap them, or press 1-4 (5 uses your prize)
- Missile, Heat, Freeze, Scissors and Paint: pick the boost, then click or tap a slime in the jar
- Pause: Esc or P
- 2 players on one device: Player 1 uses A/D to move and S to drop; Player 2 uses Left/Right to move and Down to drop. On touch screens, each player uses one half of the screen.

## How to play

1. Drop slimes into the jar.
2. Two slimes of the same size merge into the next level. Merge three or more at once to skip a level.
3. Merge again quickly for combos and more points.
4. Use boosts when the jar gets crowded: Swap trades the slime in your hand for the next one, Shake shakes the jar, Widen makes the jar wider (once per run) and Missile blows up the slime you pick.
5. Out of a boost? Get +1 by watching a video or by spending drops.
6. Reach level 14 to make a Super Slime, then keep going for more.
7. Earn drops by merging, discovering new slimes, completing missions and keeping your daily streak, and spend them in the shop.
